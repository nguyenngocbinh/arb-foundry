Research · Risk-first · Paper trading

Arb Foundry

A modular foundation for researching cross-exchange crypto arbitrage without placing real orders.

Safety boundary: live order submission is not implemented. This page describes the target architecture, not a claim that all components are already available.

Target data flow

1. Exchange adaptersBinance · OKX · Bybit · KuCoin — public market data first
↓
2. Market data normalizationTicker, order book, symbol mapping, UTC timestamps, freshness and depth validation
↓
3. Opportunity scannerGross/net spread after fees, executable-size VWAP and stale-data rejection
↓
Classic spot–spotCompare buy and sell venues while accounting for transfer and inventory constraints.
Spot–perpetualResearch funding, basis, margin and liquidation risks; no live execution.
↓
4. Risk engine + paper simulatorSimulated fills, fees, slippage, latency, positions, PnL, limits and kill conditions
↓
5. Persistence, reporting and monitoringReproducible evaluations, 24-hour API health checks, logs and failure analysis

Implementation phases

  1. Project foundation, tests, lint and CI.
  2. Public market-data adapters and 24-hour API health checks.
  3. Depth-aware spot arbitrage scanner and net-spread calculation.
  4. Separate strategy modules for spot–spot and spot–perpetual research.
  5. Paper trading, risk limits, persistence and recovery tests.
  6. Evaluation reports, operational guidance and documentation.

Engineering rules

  • Paper trading only by default; no secrets in Git.
  • Reject stale data and insufficient order-book depth.
  • Every milestone requires automated tests and traceable evidence.
  • Changes stay on feature branches; merging into main requires explicit approval.
